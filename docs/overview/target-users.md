## eLoop Users & Pain Points

| User Type | Role | Pain Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Recycling Center Operator** | Registers device intakes, scans components at the sorting line, confirms individual classifications, and clicks "Finish Batch" to close out and list a batch. | • Slow, manual intake bottlenecks.<br>• Visual guesswork instead of accurate material pricing.<br>• Low visibility into true value of e-waste piles. |
| **Refinery Worker** | Browses the Digital Showroom, filters batches by value tier, and books batches for procurement. | • Lack of an organized marketplace for recycled minerals.<br>• High search friction and unpredictable feedstock volumes.<br>• High risk of procuring unverified, low-grade batches. |
| **Admin** | Holds elevated access across the platform; manages user permissions, profiles, and corporate company accounts. | • Fragmented oversight of multi-facility data streams.<br>• High administrative friction when onboarding new business tenants.<br>• Difficulty tracking platform-wide lifecycle compliance. |
