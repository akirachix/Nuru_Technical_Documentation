## The problem eLoop solves

The green transition has brought about an unprecedented demand for critical minerals for clean energy such as lithium and cobalt. But the existing electronic waste recovery system is plagued by high inefficiencies.


## The Market Disconnect

*   **Sourcing Hazards:** Traditional mining operations result in serious human, environmental, and geopolitical drawbacks at both legal and illegal mines.
*   **Low-Visibility Processing:** Local recycling plants process tons and tons of e-waste without knowledge of what they are. They have no diagnostic tools for quantifying the rare-earth distribution and depend totally on visual estimation.
*   **Sourcing Fragmentation:** Industrial refineries may have the metallurgical facilities to separate pure elements from the scrap but have not got access to a centralized, verified, or predictable repository.


## The eLoop Solution

E-Loop fills this void, bringing Edge Intelligence to the world of e-waste, making it a B2B commodity that can be accessed and searched.

```text
 [Raw Ingest] ──> [Edge AI Analysis] ──> [Batch Valuation] ──> [Digital Showroom]
 Guesswork        PCBs & Batteries       HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW   Refinery Procurement
```

*   **Ingestion Points and Automated Valuation:** Instead of subjective estimates, consistent asset profiles are created with software-backed ingestion points.
*   **Granular Edge Analysis:** Individual components are classified using real-time computer vision, and then they are grouped into aggregate batch values (**HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW**).
*   **Centralized B2B Sourcing:** Each verified listing will automatically be added to a secure Digital Showroom, allowing refineries to easily browse, filter, and book target feedstocks.
*   **Unified Lifecycle Tracking:** Simplifies complex downstream procurement with a single state tracking engine:
* **Batch Status:** Shows when a batch is `In Progress` → `Listed` → `Booked` → `Completed`.
* **Booking Status:** Tracks the status of all bookings from `In Progress` → `Confirmed` or `Cancelled`.
